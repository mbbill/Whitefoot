Decision: A fact state records which part of its bound matrix is already its [ENT-4] closure, and closing the state inserts only the relations added since and the implicit bounds of the terms registered or killed since, and rederives the cells whose bound or disequality became weaker or absent when proof candidates were removed while both terms survive; it falls back to a fixed point seeded from the recorded closed part, which accepts only strictly smaller bounds and absent disequalities, when more cells were weakened than the state has terms or their rederivation does not settle, and to the complete unseeded fixed point when nothing is recorded as closed or the closure leaves out a term, because the [incremental closure study](../../research/investigations/proof-certificate-architecture/INCREMENTAL-CLOSURE.md) measures the cubic closure recomputed at every kill, join and query as the dominant checking cost of real programs, finds a compiler with this node's three decisions and smaller changes to how proof candidates are stored and shared checking its two largest real programs 21 to 28 times faster and three smaller ones 4 to 9 times faster, and finds bounds, disequalities and contradictions identical to the complete closure wherever a test-only check recomputed every closure completely, on utf8parse, the raw DEFLATE chain, those two programs and generated flows, instead of recomputing the complete closure at every closure point. The record tracks every kill and join, so it retires the refusals of a cross-flow closure cache in compiler/proof-query-context and compiler/closure-row-dominance.

Decision: When several derivations prove the same L0 bound, which one a state retains, and with it the order and identities of derivation ledger entries, is left to the closure that found it, because [ENT-4] fixes derivability and dispositions but not that witness, and keeping the complete traversal's witness is what forced the cubic recomputation, instead of keeping every selected derivation identical to the complete traversal. A join reuses a proof selected by every non-contradictory predecessor only when every predecessor contributes and that proof is already an independently live conclusion or an implicit fact; a temporary transitive or strengthened consequence still receives a join boundary before a later kill can remove its premises. Ordinary fallback candidates, which exclude removable callee-postcondition dependencies, are preserved where the selected proof needs them, including across delivery substitution and its join, because equal selected values alone do not establish equal behavior after candidate removal. A contradictory predecessor's proof remains retained but contributes no removable value dependency, because ENT-5 makes that predecessor absorbing and neutral. This retires compiler/closure-row-dominance's refusal of replacing the witness-selection algorithm, which rested on keeping that witness.

Decision: The selected bounds of a fact state and every closed view are dense matrices, and the closed view of an unchanged state is remembered, because once closure is incremental the same study attributes the next costs to rebuilding hashed maps, sorting their keys and closing one unchanged state repeatedly, instead of hashed bound maps rebuilt at every closure, materialization and join. The remembered view withdraws two refusals proposed earlier in the compiler/closure-evaluation amendment. The first shared one closure between the check that turns a contradictory state into the absorbing contradictory state before a kill and the materialization of that unchanged state for the kill; it was refused for a measured 1.04x gain that needed code only for that check, and the remembered view now hands the check's closure to the materialization without such code. The second shared each predecessor's closure between the same check before a join and the join itself; it was refused because the shared closures created ledger entries in a different order, which the previous decision no longer fixes.

Rejected:
- A second production difference-bound implementation with its own kill rules: rejected because term kills and candidate removals already have one flow implementation that records changed terms and cells; duplicating that rule would add an independent source of acceptance behavior. Test-only eager snapshots and joins advance their own states to check transition preservation, in addition to recomputing incremental closures from the optimized state's facts.
- Sharing matrix rows between the states that meet at a join: rejected because a kill or materialization in one arm rewrites a column in every row, so few rows would still be shared at the join.
- Storing only bounds stronger than the bound their terms' type ranges imply through zero: rejected because removing postcondition proofs at a kill, joining states and delivering relations to a receiver would each need a separate argument for the omitted cells, and no measurement shows that the remaining cost needs it.
