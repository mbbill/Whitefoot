Decision: Reuse the ordinary L0 closure and ordered affine index across queries with the same entering facts and value images, and closed/type interval endpoints within one affine candidate traversal, rebuilding views when term or goal information changes, because the [query-cost study](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/e84912f215072419093e66413b21e082dab36400/research/investigations/proof-certificate-architecture/CHECKING-COST.md#query-preparation-selection) isolates repeated preparation, instead of recomputing each premise or materializing new live facts for reuse. Candidate and diagnostic-parent traversal stay ordered within the prepared view; its L0 witness follows [incremental closure](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/main/design/compiler/incremental-closure.md).
