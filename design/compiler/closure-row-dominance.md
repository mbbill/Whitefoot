Decision: Skip a complete transitive candidate product only when conservative numeric and proof-depth row bounds prove that none of its candidates can improve a fully populated destination row, retaining the original traversal for every undecided product and equal-depth tie, because the [entering-context cost study](../../research/investigations/proof-certificate-architecture/CHECKING-COST.md#entering-context-follow-up) attributes substantial work to closure traversal and measures the benefit of applying its existing scalar rejection conditions to whole products without changing the accepted updates or selected derivations, instead of visiting every non-improving product, introducing a cross-flow closure cache, or replacing the witness-selection algorithm.
