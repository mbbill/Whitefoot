Decision: Each worker thread owns a fixed-capacity deque of offer slots, an offer is a push onto the offering thread's own deque, idle threads steal the oldest entry, and a join reclaims its own offer and runs it inline before waiting on a stolen one, because an offer under the lane-scan design paid a contended scan over every lane and a mutex-and-condvar handshake whether granted or refused, up to 48.6 times slower than sequential at fine grain, while a per-thread deque makes an offer two local stores and moves all synchronization cost to steals, whose count follows imbalance rather than offer rate, instead of a shared array of lanes scanned per offer.

Decision: A full deque refuses the offer and the call runs inline, so refusal falls on the deepest pending fork and the capacity doubles as a grain floor with no tuned constant, because rate-limiting promotion could bound refusal cost but could neither lift the ceiling at coarse grain, where overhead is already amortized, nor unblock the skew shape, where the caller sleeps on the half it handed out, and promoting the oldest pending fork point requires retaining fork points, which is a deque, instead of a heartbeat promotion gate.

Decision: A waiting join works instead of sleeping, draining its own deque and then stealing, and parks only when no work exists anywhere, because a sleeping joiner wastes the thread the program already has, instead of a join that sleeps on a condition variable.

Decision: Compute tasks run on persistent native worker threads and their ordinary stacks, a join executes an unstolen target on the joining thread, a stolen target lets the joining thread run and steal other compute work before a bounded spin, yield, and condition-wait slow path, a may-suspend user call stays on its caller's stack and is never published to a compute worker, and an I/O join progresses completion and then waits through the native backend on that same stack without helping compute tasks, because parking the joining stack at a compute miss measured eight to nine percent behind at four and eight workers and could only be trusted through a switchable-stack pool, a managed-stack state machine, and an exhaustive schedule enumerator, and helping from an I/O join would need those switchable stacks too, instead of a park-on-miss scheduler that resumes a parked stack on whichever worker finishes its target and reaps every I/O join through the compute runtime.

Decision: The deque stress probe, run under `make -C compiler sched-deque-test` and repeated under ThreadSanitizer, is the runtime's concurrency check, with separate startup, join, completion, and exhaustion tests for the boundaries it does not reach, because the retired enumerator modeled the managed-stack state machine that no longer exists, and a probe on native threads finds races in the executions it observes without claiming the exhaustive coverage the enumerator had, instead of keeping an enumerator of schedules the runtime no longer takes.

Decision: Every worker thread runs on a stack the same size as the entry's, one runtime-owned constant of one gibibyte exported across the language boundary rather than the environment's limit, reserved rather than committed so a program that never recurses deeply pays nothing for the headroom, because a lane sized from the environment reintroduced on the lanes the dependence the entry's fixed size had removed, left a lane two orders of magnitude shorter than the entry, and made whether a deep recursion survived depend on which thread stole it, while raising the reservation costs only address space and a runaway recursion should still end in bounded time, instead of inheriting the environment's stack limit.

Decision: A lane with no work stays hot for an idle window in time, `WF_PAR_IDLE_WINDOW_US` of 1,000 microseconds sampled once per 1,024 spin rounds off a monotonic microsecond clock, taken only when the pool's lanes at start are at or below the CPUs the process may run on as read from its affinity mask, while an oversubscribed pool, or one that cannot learn its CPU count, keeps the fixed bound of 1,024 rounds and 16 yields, the round bound itself sized to the park-and-wake and the scan round that the probe in `sched/core.c` measures on each host and records beside the constant, because no fixed round count serves both regimes, every larger bound gaining one to eight percent of wall where the pool fits and regressing three of four kernels by 16 to 570 percent at eight lanes on four CPUs, a round is not a portable unit whereas the evidence is a duration against a park-and-wake, and 1,000 microseconds sits two orders of magnitude above that park-and-wake and one below the settling time measured for a woken lane, instead of a larger fixed spin constant.

Decision: The independent-map splitter's work unit is 150,000 and its oversubscription cap 16 chunks per lane, both compile-time constants an A/B build can override, because at eight lanes the work term and not the cap fixed the Mandelbrot kernel at 16 chunks, leaving a trailing chunk the size of the critical path, and the finer unit measured 0.692 of the wall there with five of five pairs lower, while raising the cap alone bought 18 to 19 percent more CPU for no wall at four lanes, instead of the 1,200,000 unit or a raised cap.

Decision: A group's compute members are joined newest-published-first while its completion members keep their published positions, because the deque is Chase-Lev, so a join taken newest first finds its target at the owner's end or already stolen and never buried under a newer entry, and join order is unobservable since every value is fixed to its source-order result, instead of joining in publish order.

Rejected:
- A fixed array of worker lanes scanned with a compare-and-swap per lane and a mutex-condvar handshake: rejected because every offer paid the scan and the handshake whether granted or refused, 48.8 nanoseconds per fork at the coarsest cell and up to 48.6 times slower than sequential at fine grain.
- A heartbeat gate rate-limiting promotion by local work since the last promotion: rejected because it bounds refusal cost but cannot lift the coarse ceiling where overhead is already amortized, cannot unblock the skew shape where the caller sleeps on the half it handed out, and promoting the oldest pending fork requires retaining fork points, which is a deque.
- Parking the joining stack at a compute miss and resuming it on whichever worker finishes the target: rejected because it measured eight to nine percent behind at four and eight workers on the layout program and could only be trusted through a switchable-stack pool, a managed-stack state machine, and an exhaustive schedule enumerator.
- A lane stack sized as the larger of the environment's limit and a fixed floor: rejected because it reintroduced environment dependence on the lanes and made a deep recursion's survival depend on which thread stole it.
- A wake-path change for the sparse-cadence loss seen on the hosted SMT runner class: rejected because the loss does not reproduce across machines of that class that report identical topology, their baseline core speed differs by 18 percent, and hot lanes over parked ones are worth 1.6 to 6.5 percent of wall for 7 to 11 percent more CPU there, so that class is not a fixed instrument for a ten percent question.
