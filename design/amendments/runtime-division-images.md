Decision: An admitted unsigned exact division over terms or constants publishes the quotient's bound for runtime divisors as well as literals, and an independently admitted exact product of the same quotient and divisor values publishes its bound by the captured dividend, because runtime block decomposition needs those elementary consequences of its actual arithmetic and they can be checked by finite value-image matching without nonlinear search, instead of limiting quotient facts to written literal divisors, requiring callers to supply an already-proved partition, or inserting tests for relations that must hold. The existing literal scaled image remains; ordinary product-domain discharge is still required. The consumer, alternatives, and boundaries are in research/investigations/compute-model/DESIGN.md#runtime-division-images.
