Decision: Result transport reuses completed numeric closure through the existing copy-on-write fact stores, preparing the ordinary snapshot once per shared flow point, using its closed core when it covers more registered terms than the conditional core, and retaining the unaffected core during payload substitution, because the measured DEFLATE regression and independent-outcome and join costs come from repeating completed closure and snapshot work, instead of rebuilding transported matrices as unseeded states or selecting a smaller predicate vocabulary. Every context remains isolated and imports the complete numeric candidate set, including independently live ordinary fallbacks; writer origins and opaque goals do not travel with a Result, and the existing insertion and kill records track every affected cell. Core size selects reuse, not derivability or a work limit, and an equal-bound witness may change. This supplements the isolated-context decision in compiler/checker-facts and the closure-record decision in compiler/incremental-closure; the correspondence argument and matched cost criteria are in research/investigations/result-proof-transport/DESIGN.md.

Rejected:
- Projecting a context to selected payload bounds or ending it at a guessed last use in this change: rejected because either changes which facts survive later events or adds a new lifetime analysis, while preserving the existing closed core has a smaller correspondence obligation and a direct measured consumer.
