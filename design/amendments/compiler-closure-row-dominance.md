Node: compiler/closure-row-dominance

Decision: Prune a fixed-point transitive candidate product only when conservative numeric and proof-depth row bounds prove that it cannot improve a fully populated destination row, preserving the unpruned accepted updates and, for unseeded closure, its selected derivations on undecided products and equal-depth ties, because the [row comparison](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/b3341e32ab8d69e2974227aee7146ac16eb2fe00/research/investigations/proof-certificate-architecture/CHECKING-COST.md#entering-context-follow-up) isolates savings from applying scalar rejection conditions to whole products, instead of visiting every non-improving product. Seeded closure applies the same pruning to strictly improving products under the witness policy in [incremental closure](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/main/design/compiler/incremental-closure.md).
