Decision: A subscript offset that reads a binding carries no occurrence identity inside a term: two subscripted measure places rooted at one declaration and spelled alike are one term, because [ENT-2] states that "Two places are the same term exactly when their roots resolve to the same declaration event and their canonical source spellings are byte-identical", and [MSR-2] puts "the support of every offset occurring in P" into every enclosing measure term, so a write to the offset binding kills every term the offset occurs in rather than silently retargeting one, instead of keying such an offset on the occurrence that evaluated it, which made the same written place two terms and left a clause's `deref(rows)[i].len` unable to discharge the body's own obligation over the same spelling.

Decision: The occurrence is still what decides [OWN-7]'s separation question for two binding-valued offsets, because term identity "under-approximates aliasing, while kills use [OWN-7]'s resolved-place overlap relation and over-approximate it" [ENT-2], and two reads of one binding straddling a write to it may select two storages, instead of dropping the occurrence in both relations, which would take two places formed on either side of a write to be one storage.

Decision: A subscript written inside a `contract_block` measure place whose offset is a value parameter of the same callable is retained as that formal's ordinal and substituted at each reader  -  the caller's instantiation by its own actual, the callee body by that parameter's binding  -  because a declaration-boundary template names a parameter by position and never by a binding, and [EFF-5] already substitutes a row's written index positions by the actual at the call, instead of storing a body binding in the template, which names nothing at a caller, or leaving the whole clause an unimplemented composite value, which refused `requires k < deref(rows)[i].len;` outright.

Rejected:
- Admitting only a literal or const offset in a clause measure place: rejected because [MSR-1] names "a live `own` fragment-integer place" beside them, and it is the binding-valued offset that makes `table[i].len` provable for the element a loop is at.
