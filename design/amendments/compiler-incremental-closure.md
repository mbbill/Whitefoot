Node: compiler/incremental-closure

Decision: A fact state records its completed ENT-4 closure, inserts new relations and fresh or killed terms' implicit bounds, and repairs cells weakened by candidate removal while both terms survive, because the [closure study](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/b3341e32ab8d69e2974227aee7146ac16eb2fe00/research/investigations/proof-certificate-architecture/INCREMENTAL-CLOSURE.md#implementation) identifies repeated complete closure as the dominant cost, instead of recomputing it after every kill, join and query. More weakened cells than terms or unsettled repair use a seeded fixed point admitting only strictly smaller bounds and absent disequalities; no recorded closure or a missing term uses the complete unseeded fixed point.

Decision: Equal L0 bounds may retain different valid derivations and ledger identities, because ENT-4 fixes derivability rather than witness choice, instead of forcing the complete traversal's witness. A join reuses a shared selected proof only when every non-contradictory predecessor contributes it and it is already independently live or implicit; temporary consequences still receive a join boundary. Ordinary fallback candidates survive removable postcondition dependencies, including delivery substitution and its join. A contradictory predecessor retains its proof but contributes no removable value dependency, because ENT-5 makes it absorbing and neutral. The [retained-proof argument](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/b3341e32ab8d69e2974227aee7146ac16eb2fe00/research/investigations/proof-certificate-architecture/INCREMENTAL-CLOSURE.md#retained-derivations) explains why equal numeric values alone do not preserve later kills.

Decision: Selected bounds and closed views use dense matrices, with an unchanged state's closed view remembered, because the [representation attribution](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/b3341e32ab8d69e2974227aee7146ac16eb2fe00/research/investigations/proof-certificate-architecture/INCREMENTAL-CLOSURE.md#implementation) identifies repeated map construction, key sorting and closure of unchanged states as remaining costs, instead of rebuilding hashed bounds at each closure, materialization and join.

Rejected:
- A second production difference-bound implementation with its own kill rules: rejected because term kills and candidate removals already have one flow implementation that records changed terms and cells; duplicating that rule would add an independent source of acceptance behavior. Test-only eager snapshots and joins advance their own states to check transition preservation, in addition to recomputing incremental closures from the optimized state's facts.
- Sharing matrix rows between the states that meet at a join: rejected because a kill or materialization in one arm rewrites a column in every row, so few rows would still be shared at the join.
- Storing only bounds stronger than the bound their terms' type ranges imply through zero: rejected because removing postcondition proofs at a kill, joining states and delivering relations to a receiver would each need a separate argument for the omitted cells, and no measurement shows that the remaining cost needs it.
