Node: design/language/data-model/vector-consumption.md

Decision: The reusable prefix-window vector consumes a removed suffix synchronously through a monomorphized member in the elements' original order, preserves capacity and the retained prefix, and implements that consumption by reversing the suffix and taking from the back, because the ordinary effect-overlap rule excludes the member from observing the mutated backing, the composition requires no scratch allocation or invalid slot, and its linear element movement removes repeated front-removal's quadratic cost; the extra transfers against a direct native consumer must still be measured on scalar and large owned elements before claiming competitive cost, instead of repeated front removal, an implicit drop of a must-consume element, or a resumable drain object with stored references.
