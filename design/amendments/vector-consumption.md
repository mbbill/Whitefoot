Decision: The reusable prefix-window vector consumes a removed suffix synchronously through a monomorphized member in the elements' original order, preserving capacity and the retained prefix, and uses suffix reversal followed by back consumption as its current O(n) baseline, because the ordinary effect-overlap rule excludes the member from observing the mutated backing, the composition needs no scratch allocation or invalid slot, and the [matched native comparison](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/34ede1dedd278652ce153452fc305caf241c45c0/research/experiments/container-representation/vector-library/RESULTS.md) establishes the remaining extra-transfer cost instead of establishing minimum-transfer performance; a performance-critical ordered consumer reopens the representation or operation choice under the same ownership and callback contract, instead of retaining quadratic front removal, implicitly dropping must-consume elements, or storing escaping references in a resumable drain object.
