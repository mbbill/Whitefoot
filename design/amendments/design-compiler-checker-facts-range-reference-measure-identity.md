Decision: The one measure a range reference has is a term over the reference place itself  -  the binding a range formation bound, with no step below it  -  and never over the storage the range was formed over, because [ENT-2] clause (b) admits `deref(view)` as a measure place, a root carrying "field-selection and enum-payload `psuffix`es, `deref` wrappings, and subscripts", and admits no place formed with a range step, while [REF-4] makes the measure `hi - lo`, a quantity the storage below the range does not have, instead of resolving the actual through the reference at a call and reading `deref(part).len` as the owner's own `len`, which admitted an index outside the range and read outside the array.

Decision: A `&[T]` actual that forms its range at the call keeps that formation's range step over the resolved storage path, as one ordered projection of the goal datum, because such a range names no binding and so has no [ENT-2] place of its own, and the step is what keeps its `len` distinct from the `len` of the storage it was formed over, instead of dropping the step onto that storage, which is the same collapse, or refusing the actual outright, which would refuse `f(edges: &edges[0_u64..12_u64])`  -  a shape the corpus writes and [REF-4] admits.

Decision: The `len` of such an anonymous range is the mathematical difference of its two captured endpoints where both are value-determined, taken as the pre-transfer term [MSR-3] gives that operand, because [REF-4] states the measure "equal to `hi - lo`" and [REF-1] makes both endpoints immutable values captured at the formation, instead of an opaque measure term over the range path, which carries no fact and leaves every requirement over a written range unproved.

Decision: A range formation establishes its length equality in the ordinary L0 relation state as well as in the affine measure image, whenever the mathematical difference is one term displaced by a constant, because [ENT-3.S6] states that `let part = &P[lo..hi];` "establishes `deref(part).len = hi - lo`" as a fact like any other and [ENT-4]'s fragment represents exactly that shape, instead of leaving it only as an affine premise, which left `deref(view).len == 20_u64` unproved at a caller that had just formed the range.

Rejected:
- Keying every range measure on the resolved storage path with its range step, so that a binding-named range and an anonymous one are one term: rejected because [ENT-2] decides term identity by the written place, and the place a writer writes for a bound range is `deref(view)`; a term the writer cannot spell cannot be named in the diagnostic that reports its own residual [OP-15].
- Refusing an actual that forms its range at the call, on the ground that it names no [ENT-2] place: rejected because [REF-4] admits the formation in argument position and the measure it owes is a constant whenever the endpoints are written, so the refusal would reject provable programs the corpus already contains.
