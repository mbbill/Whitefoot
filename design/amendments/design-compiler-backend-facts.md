Node: compiler/backend-facts

Decision: The backend may supply the checker's already-proved facts as target attributes, instruction flags, metadata or assumptions, every supplied fact reading a derivation the checked program retains and none adding a runtime branch, lock, dependency or scheduling edge, because [DIAG-2] permits all four forms while requiring unchanged source verdicts and program behavior without them, and retained disjointness need not be rediscovered by the optimizer, instead of withholding proved information or treating an optimizer assertion as an independent source of proof.

Decision: Each fact family is admitted only when retained evidence establishes its complete target contract: reference attributes require the corresponding validity, extent, capture and alias guarantees; memory-effect attributes also account for memory outside parameter paths; address and arithmetic flags require their exact domain facts; and scoped alias or loop-access metadata requires the complete accesses covered by the retained separation proof, because a source rule can establish part of an LLVM promise without establishing all of it, as an effect row omits allocation and `swap` permits aliases, instead of treating the names of source rules as sufficient authority for a blanket emitted set. The emitted-set amendment defines the implemented subset and its specific boundaries; other eligible families remain absent until their complete mappings are available.

Decision: `noalias` goes on reference parameters governed by the ordinary [EFF-5] check, including a parameter the row only reads, with source-permitted alias exceptions such as `swap` excluded, because that check runs at every call and proves the substituted paths disjoint wherever at least one writes, matching the attribute's restriction on locations modified during the call, instead of withholding the attribute solely because the source proof is established at callers or applying it to an operation that permits the aliases it forbids.

Decision: An `llvm.assume` is eligible under the same retained-proof and zero-runtime-cost conditions as other supplied facts, but no fact is emitted when its full derivation or target mapping is missing, because [DIAG-2] expressly permits assumptions and does not make optional optimizer information a condition of acceptance or target qualification, instead of retaining the superseded blanket prohibition or asserting an unproved condition to the optimizer.

Rejected:
- Starting with `nsw` from discharged integer-domain obligations alone and deferring `noalias` on the ground that [EFF-5] proves disjointness at a call while `noalias` promises something about every caller: rejected because the checker performs that call-site proof at every call and compiles no call whose proof fails, so the callee-side promise is the conjunction of proofs the language already demands, and the attribute constrains only locations modified during the call, which is precisely where [EFF-5] demands disjointness.
- Emitting an unproved assumption or using optional optimizer facts as an alternate acceptance or target-qualification path: rejected because [DIAG-2] permits only facts the checker discharged and requires both judgments to remain unchanged with facts off.
- Treating the emitted attributes as a source-visible guarantee, or naming them at the surface: rejected because they are lowering consequences of invariants the specification states in its own vocabulary, and a surface name that borrows backend vocabulary labels the consequence rather than the invariant.
