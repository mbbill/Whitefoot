Decision: The written place's trailing measure step is what selects this reading, and the place it names must be the measured place itself, because [MSR-2] separates descriptor storage from element storage and states the element sentence over storage rather than over the word *element*, so a measure word of `P[i]` is descriptor storage of that element and reaches no measure of P; the step list is therefore cut at its first measure step and the remaining path must name the supported place exactly, instead of treating any written place below P as a descriptor write, which would kill an element's measures from a write that reaches only P's own words.

Decision: Such a write reaches exactly the word it names and the measures the table derives from that word, so `writes(window.len)` kills `len` and the derived `room` and leaves `cap` and `head` alive, because [EFF-2]'s both-ways check is what admits the row and a body that changed another word of P's descriptor would exhibit a write the row does not carry, so the checker may not assume an undeclared word moved; killing `cap` as well forced every runtime-capacity window proof to relate the entry and exit capacity atoms through the operation's transported `cap` before it could use the entry room at all, which is one premise more than [ENT-6]'s AUTO families reach, instead of killing every measure of P, which is the whole-descriptor reading [MSR-2]'s support sentence suggests but which no [PRE-1] row's declared effect justifies.

Decision: A window row's `room` measure carries no affine atom of its own: its image is the difference of the place's `cap` and `len` images, because [MSR-1]'s table writes the cell as `cap - len, exact` for every `Slots` and `Ring` row exactly as it writes `Array<T>`'s `cap` cell as `len`, and [ENT-6] reads a measure observation as "a standing constant or the captured range image where those rules fix it"; with an independent atom the only thing relating the three was [MSR-2]'s identity as two affine premises, and a proof that had to cross one window operation then needed that identity over the retired atoms, the identity over the fresh ones, and the operation's own transported `len` at once, which is three premises against [ENT-6]'s two-premise ceiling and left the whole `invariant spare: r.room + at >= n` family underivable. With the image fixed by the table the identity carries no term at all, [ENT-6]'s own "already trivial" case, and every such proof is ordinary difference-bound work over `len`.

Decision: The [ENT-4] closure receives `room <= cap` from that cell and nothing else, because `room = cap - len` relates three terms and no difference-bound domain can hold it, while the ordering it implies is true at every program point since a length is never negative, instead of publishing nothing, which would drop a bound the closure can use, or attempting to encode the identity itself, which the domain cannot represent.
