Decision: Checked evidence is retained in per-component fragments, each finalized once when its producer succeeds, and composition assembles the selected program's receipt from validated references to current fragments, the receipt alone granting lowering authority, while a failed build keeps unrelated valid fragments and an incompatible, missing or incomplete cache entry causes recomputation rather than rejection, because, as the [cache publication design](../../research/investigations/modular-compilation/DESIGN.md#cache-authority-and-publication) records, re-traversing and remapping every derivation of the whole program after each edit would defeat proof-level incrementality, and a failure elsewhere does not invalidate a proof whose inputs are unchanged, instead of one checked-program value that owns and discards all evidence of the exact compilation unit, or discarding retained work after any failed build.
