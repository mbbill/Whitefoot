Decision: Exactly one boundary-construct family, covering unsafe regions, foreign extern frames, and trusted primitive imports, shares one per-fact soundness-obligation ledger, a member without a manifest is unrepresentable, and authoring a member is a privileged, human-approved operation the ordinary writer cannot perform, because separate escape mechanisms would let performance pressure arbitrage between them, and keeping trust inside the language preserves a partial verifiability that opaque foreign code forfeits, instead of separate unsafe, foreign-interface, and primitive-import mechanisms, or all trust placed outside the language.
