Decision: The grammar is deterministic, unambiguous, and context-free, recognized by a generated strong LL(2) parser whose lexer decides a terminal from token shape and exact spelling alone and whose every choice point resolves within two tokens, with no desugaring between the parse tree and the checked tree, because the language is meant to be as simple as it can be and to compile fast, and a grammar made richer for the comfort of a human writer buys nothing here, instead of unbounded-lookahead recursive descent, PEG, GLR, LALR tables, Pratt parsing, or keyword-priority lexing.
