Decision: A counted loop is judged for parallel permission in its own right under its own rule, because its permission must not depend on manufacturing a sibling-call pair in source, and stating the loop conditions inside the pair rule interleaved two quantifications that read badly while a separate rule keeps the byte surface an owner reviews at activation minimal, instead of an amendment folded into the sibling-call rule.

Rejected:
- Counted-loop permission as an amendment inside the sibling-call overlap rule, quantified over iterations: rejected because the pair conditions and the iteration-quantified conditions read badly interleaved in one rule, and a separate rule keeps the reviewed byte surface minimal.
