Decision: A counted loop's permission admits at most one accumulator combined by one operation from a fixed associative set, and enclosing storage writes use either one proved constant-coefficient affine element map per resolved root or a proved exclusive subrange of an adjacent stride partition with loop-invariant runtime stride and base, consuming retained source proofs, because reductions delivered the measured parallel win and runtime-width stencil rows and block-local workspaces need ordinary helper calls on ranges whose independence does not depend on worker count, instead of unbounded multi-accumulator reductions, limiting every enclosing write to one element map, or general index search. Whole-source accesses to mapped or assigned storage and incompatible maps of overlapping storage still deny permission; arbitrary scatter is not admitted. The range argument and its limits are in research/investigations/range-loans/DESIGN.md.
