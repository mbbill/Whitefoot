Decision: A counted loop's permission admits at most one accumulator combined by one operation from a fixed associative set, and separately one element write per resolved root through a proved affine offset that reuses the retained bounds proof, with no stencil, no whole-root access, and no second map of one root, because the reduction delivered the measured parallel win while element-write permission at real per-element cost measured as a loss before the language could name disjoint elements, instead of unbounded multi-accumulator reductions or a general index-aware overlap relation.
