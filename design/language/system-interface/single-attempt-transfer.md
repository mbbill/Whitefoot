Decision: Every range-bearing system operation makes at most one progress-producing host attempt and reports exactly what happened, with no read-exact, write-all, positioned, vectored, seek, peek, pushback, or framing operation in the inventory, because a source loop over the single-attempt primitive can inspect cancellation or signal state and accumulate progress and a terminal reason itself, which a built-in looping form would hide behind a convenience, instead of composite operations that loop to completion inside the system interface.
