Stated 2026-08-06: the owner is not doing language design for its own sake and
does not consider themselves a language-design expert. Whitefoot exists because
"我主要是想给我后面的项目搭一个更好的基石，目前我能看到的语言都不能让我满意" —
a better foundation for their own future projects, since no existing language
satisfies them.

**Why:** this calibrates every trade-off discussion: the audience is the owner's
future selves/agents building on WF, not language-research peers or adoption
metrics. They accept deep redesigns (e.g. the obligation-discharge trap
semantics, research/investigations/obligation-discharge/) when argued from
first principles, but stay suspicious of discussion euphoria — they explicitly
asked to validate on real code before believing a design ("担心到真实场景里面
试一下发现根本不work").

**How to apply:** in design debates, argue mechanism over doctrine (they
distrust both humans and AI making unenforced judgment calls), concede real
holes fast, and always land on a cheap falsifier against the real corpus
before proposing spec motion. Long adversarial dialogue in Chinese is their
preferred design mode; the output lands as an English dossier under
research/. See [[whitefoot-collaboration-model]].
