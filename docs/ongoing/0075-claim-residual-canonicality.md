## Candidate outcome

The branch now contains one implementation of claim residual canonicality. A
claim is admitted only after CLM-1 shape checking and a CLM-2 judgment that the
written predicate is true, checker-unknown before S3, representable by the
normative contribution basis, and load-bearing for at least one eligible
operation, FN-8, or complete FN-9 terminal. The judgment uses the fixed
eligible set and fresh whole-program Full-minus runs for every contribution
component and whole occurrence. One-component claims reuse the one fresh run
for both evidence roles; they do not silently omit either role.

The implementation distinguishes three exact predicate images. The direct
snapshot is the written Bool value, the support-canonical image stops at the
first stable projection/normalization frontier and owns Contrib/S3, and the
fully structural image is lifecycle-only. Admission queries the images in
stable direct/support/structural order, S3 reconstructs the support image and
then materializes the direct snapshot, and proof evidence retains all three
roles without publishing counterfactual arena identities. Exact-image and
component-manifestation contradictions are classified before overlap or
refutation; same-image proof inconsistency remains a compiler failure.

Generic source bodies receive the same ordinary admission, lifecycle,
provenance, and residual judgments as dead nongeneric bodies. Stable schema
reports contain no scratch function or nominal identity. Explicit concrete
instances discovered transitively through an uninstantiated generic are
replayed after the symbolic checkpoint, recursively reifying only truly
concrete nominal arguments, and then traverse the normal concrete pipeline.
Retained symbolic FN-8 templates reify metadata-only nominals after the
executable nominal prefix, and the checked program retains the derived-const
table needed to interpret those templates.

The checked ledger now preserves finalized direct/support/structural image
evidence, normalized component facts, component and reconstruction roots,
stable counterfactual dispositions, and terminal-only non-explosive uses.
Masked proof IDs never escape their temporary arena. Runtime semantics are
unchanged for an admitted claim: it remains a `traps` effect, lowers through the
ordinary claim instruction, executes on every dynamic reach, and is never
optimized away. Runtime-failure evidence is produced by a test-only checked-IR
mutation of a genuinely admitted claim, never by admitting a false source
claim.

The ordinary real-program corpus moved from 241 claims in 23 files to 23
genuine residuals in 8 files. Result oracles, deliberately false conditions,
and control assertions became ordinary branches, typed failures, contracts, or
counted-loop facts; checker-redundant claims were deleted; effect rows and
reverse callers were tightened. All surviving literal `because` records have
the exact five fields and an exact terminal consumer. Dormant `tests/codegen`
and historical research probes remain excluded as chartered.
