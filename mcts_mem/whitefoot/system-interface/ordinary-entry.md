- A compilation unit consists of ordinary declarations and need not contain a selected process entry. A selected entry remains source-callable under the ordinary signature and proof rules.
- The standard build profile supplies one ordinary aggregate containing invocation arguments, the working directory, standard streams and factory state (Inputs). A requested store is a separate ordinary region-branded parameter.
- The build launcher owns native process setup and status conversion. Unsupported launcher profiles are build failures, not source-language rejections.
- Every required call premise remains a proof obligation of its ordinary caller. Selection as a process entry grants no requirement exception or executable proof guard.
