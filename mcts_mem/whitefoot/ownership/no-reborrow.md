- Every ordinary borrow holder has one immutable resolved descriptor/referent root. Holders bind once and never rebind or retarget at control-flow merges. A reborrow branches a rooted lineage but never merges, retargets, or reassigns a live borrow. This singleton holder invariant is distinct from v0.17's finite possible-origin summary for the storage viewed by a direct slice; borrowing a slice descriptor carries both relations without turning either into the other.
- v0.7 admits one bounded relaxation: a statement-scoped child reborrow. A caller may write `&uniq 'c deref(h)[suffix]` (or `&'c ...`) as an argument atom of an `own`/`unit`-returning call, in a locally-introduced region confined to that statement, from a parameter or `let`-bound holder. Creating it suspends the parent for the statement and it resumes at statement end; a uniq child requires a uniq parent; overlapping uniq siblings are rejected (OWN-5/6/7). The child is unbound and non-escaping; STOR-5 makes borrows unstorable, and the receiving call's non-borrow result cannot carry one beyond the statement.
- v0.20 widens the admitted family by two forms. A mode-preserving returned reborrow — the written reborrow of a parameter or `let`-bound holder (never a match binder) as the complete return expression — rides the existing borrow-rooted creation and returned-borrow signature judgments; creation suspends the holder, and function exit precedes statement end. A borrow-mode match payload binder is the scrutinee root's arm-scoped child reborrow; creating the taken arm's binders from a uniq root suspends that root for the remainder of its region, and sibling binders over distinct fields are disjoint. Every other written non-argument reborrow occurrence is an explicit positional hard error (OWN-14).
- Everything harder stays deferred, each its own future owner-gated review: bound children, `give`-position and stored reborrows, reborrows rooted at a call-result borrow, uniq→shared downgrade, written reborrows from a match-binder parent, written grandchild chains, and arm-end resumption of a matched-through uniq root.
- Exclusive access otherwise flows by linear threading, and scattered deep writes still use returned write intents under one shallow exclusive holder (the command-buffer pattern, P1).

Rust `Iterator::Item` is non-lending: an already yielded external reference retains its declared source lifetime and need not die when the adapter/cursor is destroyed. - 2026-07-14 source-adjudicated correction: result provenance is leaf-specific rather than aggregate-wide. - 2026-07-14 source-adjudicated correction: `BR-CURSOR` is limited to opaque, sealed cursor or protocol state carrying a finite typed source-and-footprint map. Retaining an arbitrary borrow-bearing `T`, callable environment, scan state, cached item, or collection payload across calls or moves requires the separate `BR-STORED` obligation; stateful-call or cursor labels alone do not authorize such storage. - 2026-07-14 soundness correction: non-lending unique traversal permits earlier unique results to remain live across later calls only when cursor progression, structural partitioning, or one checked distinctness proof establishes pairwise-disjoint footprints. Provenance and bounded reborrowing alone do not establish sibling compatibility, and pointer inequality is never sufficient, including for empty or zero-sized referents. This requirement records the research floor without approving a production disjointness mechanism. (sourced)
- 2026-07-14 empty-footprint correction: independently produced mutable empty defaults may coexist even when their pointer values compare equal, because each view has a valid alignment and an empty access footprint. This mints neither pointer-inequality authority nor `BR-DISJOINT`; exact zero length is the safety premise, and no widening or access may follow. (sourced)
- 2026-07-14 range-provenance correction: a `RangeBounds` result region is receiver-bounded, but a `Bound<&T>` endpoint's physical root remains its pre-existing external referent rather than the receiver field that stores the reference. By-value user-defined range descriptors are separate owned state: they may contain borrow leaves, are queried only while live, are destroyed exactly once, and never become the provenance root of a returned container cursor. (sourced)
- 2026-07-14 stored-transition correction: a borrow leaf that remains live through movement, relocation, projection, yield, or abandonment preserves its exact root, while a lawful call, replacement, or clone may end that leaf and create a new one only under the declared behavior or result-provenance relation. This is a leaf-lifecycle transition, not permission to retarget a still-live leaf or borrow from temporary protocol storage. (sourced)
- 2026-07-14 hasher-reborrow correction: call-scoped reborrowing preserves the `BuildHasher S` or `Hasher H` owner, not an immutable internal leaf set. Declared behavior-effect relations alone may end or install leaves; a unique leaf moved from `S` into generated `H` cannot remain live in both, and no temporary receiver, owner field, or call frame becomes a provenance root. Generated `H` remains one owner through its calls and is destroyed exactly once. (sourced)
- 2026-07-14 shared-receiver correction: a result region may be bounded by a shared receiver even when the physical root is a receiver field, a declared external root, or static storage, and the call may still evolve unrelated receiver leaves through its declared behavior effect. Mutable projection may bounded-reborrow or transfer a unique external leaf only after the source leaf ends or enters the child reborrow; the receiver remains excluded until that child ends. A temporary receiver, address, or call frame never becomes a root merely because it bounds the result region. (sourced)
- 2026-07-14 cursor-close correction: terminal `None` does not end a source-borrowing cursor's parent authority, though an exact concrete adapter transition may retire or replace its named subcursor/epoch. Parent reuse remains rejected until cursor destruction or consuming close; proven last use may release only pure parent-borrow authority after every incompatible yielded child ends and only when no close, repair, owned-state destruction, or allocation disposition remains. If such an obligation remains, an explicit consuming close would be required, but no such production surface is selected. An owned non-lending item already moved out, including one carrying external borrow leaves, remains independent of later cursor destruction. (sourced)
