- Every function signature declares an effect row — reads/writes/allocates over named regions, plus `external`, `blocks`, and `traps` — with effect categories ordered reads, writes, allocates, external, blocks, traps; `pure` is the unique spelling of the empty row and excludes every category, and a category appears at most once in one row (EFF-1).
- Rows are checked in both directions against a syntactic exhibits relation: undeclared-but-exhibited and declared-but-unexhibited are both errors; written claims and callee effects remain exhibited independently of proof, and rows are stable under optimization.
- The computed row is the union of syntactic body occurrences and the effects of every compiler-derived release that may run on a normal control-flow edge, scoped to system resource families; `buffer`, `box`, arena, and `const` releases carry the empty row and contribute nothing (EFF-2, STOR-3).
- `external` marks a call that may observe or change state outside ordinary Whitefoot memory and `blocks` one that may block its host thread; sequential external calls retain source program order at facts-off and every optimization level, and no target-side fact proves two external calls independent or reorderable (EFF-5). Contract-conformance row equality compares the two categories by presence, like `traps` (FN-3).
- There is no global mutable state and no static region; parameter-reachable memory is the only memory a function can observe, and the row's named regions cover all of it.
- Region reads and writes are attributed to their ultimate storage origin across calls, borrows, reborrows, slices, and region-carrying actual types; local region spellings never need to escape into an enclosing signature.
- Contract definitions, requirements, and postconditions are erased signature proof metadata rather than body occurrences: they contribute no read, write, allocation, external, blocking, or trap category; a retained claim contributes `traps` where written.
- The active safe-Rust compiler checks the complete six-category row across the implemented paths: memory categories project reads/writes to ultimate storage origins, `external`/`blocks` contribute from system-operation calls and every normal-edge compiler-derived release of a system resource owner, and FN-3 conformance compares the two categories by presence. Supported system programs continue through target qualification and direct native lowering; the backend emits no effect-derived LLVM attributes, alias metadata, or `willreturn`.
- Effect-derived optimization must remain downstream of successful semantic checking and cannot change the active specification's source acceptance or facts-off behavior.
