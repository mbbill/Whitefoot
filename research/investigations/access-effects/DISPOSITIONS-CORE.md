# Dispositions CORE: every D1 to D4 family under the selected core model

Research date: 2026-09-16. For each family of [OPTIONS.md](OPTIONS.md)
sections D1 to D4: selected, absorbed by the selected model (as a variant,
sugar or a later-decision option), or refused, with the reason and the
candidate or critique that established it. This is the record of refused
alternatives for [VERDICT-CORE.md](VERDICT-CORE.md). Supersede in place.


Research date: 2026-09-16. Clerk pass over OPTIONS.md §§D1–D4 (family names and
member IDs verbatim) against VERDICT-CORE.md (the selected tuple, its six grafts
G1–G6, its four repairs P-a–P-d, its Disputed notes D-1–D-6 and its refused
candidates in §4), the twelve `core/candidate-*.md`, the twenty-four
`core/critique-*.md` and `core/judge-{1,2,3}.md`.

**Dispositions used.** *selected* — the family (or a named part of it) is an
entry of the selected tuple in VERDICT-CORE §1.1. *absorbed* — the selected
model already does the family's work under another family's vocabulary, or
carries it as a variant, as sugar, or as a recorded later decision; the row names
which. *refused* — nothing in the selected model carries it, and the reason is
given against the family's own stated tension.

A family with no candidate built around it is marked **(no candidate)** and its
reason is derived from the requirements (VERDICT-D0 §2) and the programs
(PROGRAMS.md P1–P19) directly.
